FAQ

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. No account, no server. It is included in your regular device backup.

How do I move to a new phone?

Use a device backup, or Settings → Export in the app, then Settings → Import → “Choose file” on the new device.

How is strength calculated?

Weight and reps are converted to an estimated one-rep max (Epley). Liftfolio only compares you with yourself.
